Project Objectives

The objective of this analysis was to evaluate how effectively the organization manages customer relationships, order volume, and shipping operations. The project involved assessing order trends, shipment timeliness, transportation usage, and revenue contribution across customers and regions to identify performance patterns and operational opportunities. I used SQL to structure, clean, and organize relational data across Customers, Orders, Employees, Shipping, Transport, and Products tables. SQL queries were developed to consolidate order history, calculate fulfillment metrics, and analyze shipment activity by vehicle type and time period. The curated datasets were then modeled in Power BI to create an interactive dashboard for operational monitoring and executive review.

  • Customer order activity and revenue performance
  • Order fulfillment volume and monthly trends
  • Shipment execution and transport utilization
  • Employee and operations support reporting

Results

The dataset reflects operational activity for orders processed between September 2024 and August 2025, capturing a total of 243 customer orders and an aggregate revenue value of $122.08K. Of these transactions, 200 orders were successfully shipped, highlighting a strong order-to-shipment conversion rate and demonstrating effective fulfillment execution across the logistics network.
The analysis provides visibility into geographic distribution of demand through total orders by city, illustrating order activity across multiple U.S. metropolitan regions. The leading cities with the most orders is San Francisco, with San Antonio (#2) and Columbus (#1) being the cities with the least amount of orders. This is very important because it corrlates with the results we see in the shipment department. Having a direct relationship with the type of transportation TERRA Logistics use for shipping orders with Air and Truck being the two main types of transportions used to ship orders.
Shipment trends over time further indicate operational throughput, with notable shipping activity observed across the late-summer reporting period, helping highlight seasonal or demand-driven variations within the fulfillment cycle. This is interesting but not surprising since it lines up with when we see a increase in freetime within people lives. Summertime is ususally a time people tend to shop more both online and in-person. So this make since for a warehouse to increase their work load during this time period.
