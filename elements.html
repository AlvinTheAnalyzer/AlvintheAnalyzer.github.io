My Projects

Pizza Sales Report

A data-driven analysis of pizza sales performance, highlighting key metrics such as revenue, order trends, product mix, and customer purchasing behavior. The report uncovers peak sales periods, top-selling categories, and insights that help improve menu strategy and business operations.

Electric Cars Population Report

An analytical overview of electric vehicle adoption across regions, brands, and fuel types. This project explores BEV vs. PHEV distribution, CAFV-eligible vehicles, and market trends to better understand EV growth, consumer demand, and emerging sustainability patterns.

TERRA Logisticst

A data-driven operations analysis of warehouse and logistics activity, focused on customer orders, shipping performance, and transportation utilization. The project leverages a relational SQL database to model customer, order, employee, and shipment relationships, providing insight into order volume trends, shipment fulfillment rates, and regional distribution patterns. The Power BI dashboard highlights key operational metrics, vehicle transport usage, and shipping timelines, supporting performance monitoring, logistics planning, and process optimization within a simulated warehouse environment.
