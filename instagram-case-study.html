Instagram Platform Growth Case Study

This 16-page research case study explores Instagram’s rise to dominance within the social media landscape. Through academic research, platform analytics, and trend analysis, the report examines Instagram’s sustained user growth, engagement consistency, and market positioning among competing social networks. The study highlights how visual-first content, mobile-centric design, and evolving feature expansion (such as Stories and Reels) contributed to its long-term popularity and cultural influence.

I created this report to deepen my understanding of platform growth dynamics, user behavior patterns, and digital adoption trends — approaching social media from both a research and analytical perspective.
