about

Thanks for visiting! I'm a proud Philadelphian, born and raised, with a BSBA in Information Technology & Analytics from Bloomsburg University of Pennsylvania. My curiosity runs toward true crime and the complexities of human behavior, toward books that fuel my imagination, and toward new hiking trails whenever I can find them. As an avid Eagles fan, I carry that same unyielding enthusiasm into my work — professional, but never without a little fun.

the path here

age 7

A device that felt like magic

My stepfather worked for Verizon, selling cutting-edge telecom devices — imagine FaceTime, but on something that looked like a sleek early laptop. I had to know how it worked.

age 8

My first world wide web

A Dell Optiplex arrived, and browsing the internet for the first time felt like the whole world was suddenly a click away — Poptropica, Club Penguin, IMVU, Wizard101, all of it.

age 13

Taught myself to code

A "Code for Dummies" book became my introduction to Python, CSS, and JavaScript, taught entirely on my own.

age 15

Chose law, for now

Coding got set aside to focus on a different dream — becoming a lawyer. The pull of technology never fully let go, though.

freshman year

Changed my major that night

A professor's presentation on IT & Analytics brought it all rushing back. I changed my major that same night — less a decision, more a homecoming.

data, decisions, and detours

A few themes shaped how I got here. Pick one.

leadership

Even in high school, I was sharpening my thinking through programs like Teach For America — the kind of leadership work that gave me the courage to stand behind my beliefs. Leadership, conviction, and character aren't just qualities of a good leader; they're the foundation of a good analyst too.

My focus stays the same: create data-driven solutions that don't just solve problems, but leave something that actually matters.
