Welcome to ME

Bachelor of Science in Business Administration (BSBA)

Bloomsburg University of Pennsylvania

Thanks for visiting! I'm a proud Philadelphian, born and raised in a city that shaped my love of exploration and storytelling. My interests run toward true crime and the complexities of human behavior, toward books that fuel my imagination, and toward hiking new trails or planning the next travel adventure whenever I get the chance. As an avid Eagles fan, I bring that same unyielding enthusiasm to my work — professional, but always with a little fun mixed in.

the path here

age 7

A device that felt like magic

My stepfather worked for Verizon, selling cutting-edge telecom devices — imagine FaceTime, but on something that looked like a sleek early laptop. I was determined to unravel the mystery behind how it worked, and the more I interacted with it, the deeper my curiosity grew.

age 8

My first world wide web

A Dell Optiplex arrived, and the moment I sat down in front of that screen, my world changed. Browsing the web for the first time felt like the whole world was suddenly a click away — Poptropica, Club Penguin, IMVU, Wizard101, all of it. I was hooked, not just on the games, but on the possibilities.

age 13

Taught myself to code

A "Code for Dummies" book became my introduction to Python, CSS, and JavaScript, taught entirely on my own — even while my heart was still set on becoming a lawyer.

age 15

Chose law, for now

Coding got set aside to focus on that dream. But the pull of technology never fully let go — it just waited for the right moment to come rushing back.

freshman year

Changed my major that night

A professor's presentation on IT & Analytics brought it all rushing back. That very night, I changed my major and never looked back — less a decision, more a homecoming to where I'd always belonged.

data, decisions, and detours

leadership

Even in high school, I was sharpening my thinking through programs like Teach For America — the kind of leadership work that gave me the courage to stand behind my beliefs. Leadership, conviction, and character aren't just qualities of a good leader; they're the foundation of a good analyst too.

My focus stays the same: create data-driven solutions that don't just solve problems, but leave something that actually matters.
